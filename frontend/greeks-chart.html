Delta and Gamma are derived, not fetched — Dhan publishes Greeks only in a live snapshot, so each bucket's implied volatility is solved from that bucket's real traded ATM premium and Delta/Gamma computed from it — and since their units have nothing in common with price, both are normalized to the shared 0–100% right-axis scale (hover always shows the real value, never the %). The bottom strip is OI: two bars per bucket, CE left / PE right, each the ATM strike's real open-interest change colored on its own sign (green building, red unwinding) — kept off the price scale and un-netted so a call-side buildup can't visually cancel a put-side one, and only computed while the ATM strike hasn't rolled bucket-to-bucket. A series only populates for dates inside the current contract's listing window (≈5 weeks for weeklies, ≈2 months for monthlies) since Dhan retains no expired-contract history.
