Frequently Asked Questions
How is the AI Market Bias calculated?
The bias engine combines price position vs. Value Area (VAH/POC/VAL), order flow delta, OI buildup patterns, and options Greeks into a weighted confidence score shown on the Dashboard.
What does VAH / POC / VAL mean?
Value Area High, Point of Control, and Value Area Low are Market Profile concepts marking the price range where 70% of volume traded, and the single price with the highest traded volume.
How often does the Option Chain refresh?
By default every 5 seconds during market hours. You can change this under Settings → Data & Refresh.
Can I connect a different broker?
Yes. NiftyEdge currently supports Zerodha Kite Connect, with Upstox and Angel One coming soon. Manage this under Settings → Broker & API Connection.
Are strategy signals guaranteed to be profitable?
No. Signals reflect a statistical model based on historical patterns and are not investment advice. Always size positions according to your own risk limits.
